# Dusty Galactic Outflow Simulations

These movies are from [Richie & Schneider (2026)](https://doi.org/10.3847/1538-4357/ae12e8), where we used high-resolution simulations of star-forming disk galaxies to follow dust of different sizes as it is launched into multi-phase galactic outflows by stellar feedback. We compared two models with different star formation rates and star cluster distributions to see how much dust survives sputtering on its way out of the galaxy.

# Dusty Cloud-Wind Simulations

These movies are from [Richie et al. (2024)](https://doi.org/10.3847/1538-4357/ad6a1c), where we simulated individual dusty clouds in hot galactic winds to understand how dust survives sputtering in these environments. Whether a cloud survives or is destroyed depends on how the cooling time of the gas mixed between the cloud and wind compares to the time for the wind to shear the cloud apart.
